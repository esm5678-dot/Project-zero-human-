# PROJECT ZERO HUMAN

**Can an AI-led team start a real business with zero additional cash spending?**

An open experiment launched 2026-10-26. No employees, no new paid tools, no advertising spend. Existing subscriptions are allowed but their economic value is tracked separately. Human founder retains legal responsibility and approval for customer outreach, public deployment, payments and contracts.

## Current status
- Business hypothesis: ZERO OPS, small-business inquiry management starter kit.
- Price hypothesis: KRW 49,000 (not validated).
- Paying customers: 0. Verified revenue: KRW 0.
- Offline synthetic-data prototype: implemented; the [acceptance test record](prototype/ACCEPTANCE_TESTS.md) reports 8 Chromium browser checks. It is not a live service.
- Customer validation preparation: [30 sourced interview candidates](research/2026-10-26-prospect-hypotheses.md), [5 competitor alternatives and interview kit](research/2026-10-26-validation-kit.md), [30 unsent invitation drafts](research/2026-10-26-outreach-drafts.md), and [execution report with approval gates](research/2026-10-26-execution-report.md). No outreach or interviews have occurred.
