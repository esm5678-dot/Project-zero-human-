# PROJECT ZERO HUMAN

**Can an AI-led team start a real business with zero additional cash spending?**

An open experiment launched 2026-10-26. No employees, no new paid tools, no advertising spend. Existing subscriptions are allowed but their economic value is tracked separately. Human founder retains legal responsibility and approval for customer outreach, public deployment, payments and contracts.

## Current status
- Business hypothesis: ZERO OPS, small-business inquiry management starter kit.
- Price hypothesis: KRW 49,000 (not validated).
- Paying customers: 0. Verified revenue: KRW 0.
- Prototype: not yet implemented. CI workflow committed; execution status not verified.
