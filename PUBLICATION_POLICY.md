# Public experiment policy

This is an open-source, zero-additional-spend AI startup experiment.

Publish: reusable source code, synthetic examples, agent prompts, methods, non-sensitive metrics, failures, and lessons.

Never publish: API keys, passwords, tokens, real customer personal information, unredacted correspondence, payment details, private contracts, or identifying interview notes. Public logs must use aggregate or synthetic data only.

No unapproved outreach, live deployment, charges, or contracts. Existing subscriptions may be used but must be accounted for separately as economic resources. All revenue claims require independently checkable evidence with private identifiers redacted.

Forks are independent experiments. No revenue or success is guaranteed. Respect platform terms, applicable law, and customer consent.
