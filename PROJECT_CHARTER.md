# PROJECT ZERO HUMAN — Charter

Start: 2026-10-26. Duration: 90 days. Mode: LEVEL 2 / zero additional cash spend.

## Mission
Test whether an AI-led team can identify, validate and operate a legitimate revenue-generating microbusiness with zero new spending and no employees.

## Constraints
- No paid APIs, subscriptions, hosting, ads, purchases, contractors, or external spending.
- Existing tools may be used, but track their economic cost separately.
- Never access or modify CELLEN resources.
- No customer outreach, deployment, purchases, contracts, payment setup, or use of personal data without human approval.
- Never invent customers, interviews, payments, testimonials or research evidence.
- No secrets or personal information in this repository (currently public).

## Current hypothesis
ZERO OPS: small-business inquiry management starter kit. Price hypothesis KRW 49,000; not validated.

## Stage gates
1. Synthetic-data prototype and offline tests.
2. Human approval for any outreach.
3. Validate actual demand before selling.
4. Review legal/tax/privacy requirements before payment.

## Success measures
Actual external customers, collected revenue, variable costs, human intervention minutes, autonomous task completion, errors and refunds.
